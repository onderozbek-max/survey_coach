How your data is handled

  • Stays on this computer

    Reviews and your choices are saved locally. SurveyCoach never changes RedJade.

    Wording is sent to the approved Wibey service. Never include member responses or personal information.

    Opening a link may create a test participant. The reader may answer and submit synthetic test responses; exclude them from results. Coverage is always partial.
