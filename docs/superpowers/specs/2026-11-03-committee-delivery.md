## Deliberately not claimed or fully delivered

- No actual completion/drop-off prediction, participant performance benchmark or rubric/timing calibration.
- No full-branch or measured source accessibility certification. The app's accessible controls are not evidence that the original RedJade survey meets accessibility requirements.
- Optional research brief, path-specific conditional timing and member survey ratings remain Phase 4 work.
- Watch-it-read shows the latest captured screen, not a live embedded RedJade phone or complete animated capture history.
- Change sheet records picks/kept reasons; round evidence is shown separately, rather than every historical sheet row animating into a completion checkmark.
- Sign-off is a readable summary with an appendix, not a guaranteed one-printed-page layout for arbitrarily large surveys.
- Triage is shared presentation logic, not yet fully configurable rubric YAML.
- Existing generic survey titles are not guessed; Rename is explicit and does not alter immutable captured titles.
- Hosting, multi-user authentication and vendor approval remain out of scope. Keep the server loopback-only.
